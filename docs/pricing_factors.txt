### Factors Influencing House Prices in India

Several factors play a critical role in determining the market value of a residential property in India:

1. Location and Connectivity:
   - Proximity to business hubs and IT parks.
   - Quality of road infrastructure and public transport (Metro, Bus).
   - Neighborhood safety and reputation.

2. Infrastructure and Amenities:
   - Basic: 24/7 water supply and power backup.
   - Lifestyle: Swimming pools, clubhouses, and piped gas.
   - Proximity to essential services like hospitals and schools.

3. Property Age and Quality:
   - New constructions generally command a premium.
   - Well-maintained older properties in prime locations also hold high value.
   - Construction quality and brand reputation of the builder.

4. Legal and Regulatory Factors:
   - RERA compliance is mandatory for buyer trust.
   - Clear land titles and occupancy certificates (OC).

5. Economic Factors:
   - Interest rates on home loans.
   - Local economic growth and employment opportunities.
