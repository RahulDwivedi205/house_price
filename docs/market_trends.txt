### Real Estate Market Trends in India (2024-2025)

The Indian real estate market is witnessing a significant shift towards luxury and premium housing segments. Major cities like Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Pune are seeing a surge in demand for larger apartments and villas.

Key Trends:
1. Gated Communities: There is a growing preference for gated communities that offer integrated amenities like gymnasiums, swimming pools, and round-the-clock security.
2. Sustainability: Modern home buyers are increasingly looking for eco-friendly features, including rainwater harvesting and solar energy systems.
3. Appreciation Rates: Average property values in Tier-1 cities have appreciated by 7-12% annually over the last two years.
4. Infrastructure Impact: The expansion of Metro lines and new expressways (like the Dwarka Expressway) has led to a 20-30% price hike in adjacent localities.
